*GENERAL MANAGER DASHBOARD*
Create the General Manager Dashboard.

The General Manager requires broad business/branch operational visibility and decision support.

The dashboard should sit between executive oversight and day-to-day team operations.

Primary navigation:

Business Overview
Teams & Departments
Targets
Operational Work
Performance
Approvals
Escalations
Analytics
Reports
Communication

Main dashboard should emphasize:

- overall operational target achievement
- department/team performance
- active work
- overdue workload
- workload distribution
- productivity trend
- attendance/availability health
- unresolved operational issues
- pending managerial actions

Create:
1. Business Performance Overview
2. Target Monitoring
3. Department Performance
4. Operational Exceptions
5. Workload Distribution
6. Escalation Summary
7. Analytical Insights

Provide drill-down paths from business metrics to the actual operational records.

Do not make this dashboard a copy of the Chairman dashboard.
