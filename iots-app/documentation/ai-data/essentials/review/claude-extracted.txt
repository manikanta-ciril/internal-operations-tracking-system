1. Executive Introduction
1.1 What is the Internal Operations Tracking System?
Think of a large, high-performing airport operations centre. Dozens of people must do precise jobs at the exact right moment: planes must be fuelled, baggage loaded, flight paths verified, passengers boarded, and runways cleared. If baggage handlers are delayed, the pilot cannot take off. If the air traffic controller does not know who is at which gate, chaos ensues. The Airport Director does not load bags or steer planes, but needs an executive radar screen showing every flight, any safety delays, and on-time performance across all terminals.
The CIRIL Internal Operations Tracking System (IOTS) is that exact flight operations control centre, built specifically for CIRIL—India’s premier commercial real estate alliance network.
CIRIL brings together certified commercial real estate advisory firms across major metropolitan zones (North, South, East, West, Central). Corporate clients—major multinational IT companies, banks, and retail giants—need commercial offices, warehouses, and land across different cities. When a client in Mumbai needs a 50,000 sq ft office in Bengaluru, the Mumbai team originates the requirement (Lead Giver), CIRIL Headquarters coordinates and routes it to the vetted Bengaluru team (Lead Receiver), the Bengaluru team inspects properties, prepares institutional proposals, and closes the lease. Upon closing, brokerage fees are collected and split (30% to Lead Giver, 70% to Lead Receiver), and CIRIL receives its 7.5% corporate royalty.
1.2 Why Does CIRIL Need This System?
To keep this nationwide commercial machinery operating smoothly, CIRIL requires an Internal Operations Tracking System that guarantees:
Every employee knows exactly what task they need to do today, when it is due, and what materials they need.
Every manager knows who is working, who is overloaded, who is on leave, and which tasks are falling behind before deadlines are missed.
The Chairman and Executive Leadership have complete 360-degree radar visibility over daily operational health, team output, location integrity, financial milestones, and outstanding exceptions.
Outstanding employee dedication is tracked objectively and rewarded transparently through monthly and quarterly excellence recognitions.
1.3 The Three Foundational System Layers
The entire IOTS platform is structured into three foundational layers that work in complete harmony:

Layer 1 – Authentication, Geolocation & Security Layer: No self-registration. Accounts are securely provisioned by authorised administrators. Everyone logs in through the same corporate portal. The system automatically inspects credentials, verifies the physical GPS location against authorised office boundaries, records an immutable audit log, and launches the user into their customised workspace. If an employee logs in from home or a client site, the system transparently tags the session as “Remote / Off-Site” without alarm.

Layer 2 – Management, Operations & Execution Layer: Managers allocate work, balance workloads, review submissions, and resolve blockers. Execution staff focus on delivering tasks with high quality. Tasks follow a closed-loop lifecycle—no task can simply expire. When overdue, the system triggers active manager intervention. Managerial rejection enters a structured revision cycle preserving all historical versions.

Layer 3 – Intelligence, Analytics & Reward Layer: Dashboards eliminate vanity metrics. Every graph answers a vital operational question. Predictive risk alerts highlight early warning signals before a missed deadline or client escalation occurs. Automated reporting engines generate daily digests, weekly team scorecards, and monthly executive summaries. The Performance & Reward System automatically evaluates delivery quality and rewards monthly excellence and 3-consecutive-month high performers with prestigious quarterly awards.

3.3 Employee Work Execution & Activity Logging
A critical design principle: States represent where the task sits in the governance pipeline. Work Activities are chronological records created during that state. An employee can log ten activities during a single “In Progress” state.
The four structured work activities:
Progress Percentage Updates: Slider or numeric input (0% to 100%) with a mandatory brief summary of work accomplished.
Work Notes & Context Memos: Freeform text capturing conversations, client remarks, phone call outcomes, or research findings.
Time-Spent / Effort Logging: Hours and minutes recorded against the task, enabling delivery velocity calculations.
Supporting Deliverables & Evidence: Direct file attachments (spreadsheets, PDFs, presentations, images) with automatic version indexing.
3.4 Overdue Handling: Active Managerial Intervention
In IOTS, Overdue is an actionable operational alarm, not a passive badge. When the clock crosses the deadline:
The task status transitions automatically to OVERDUE.
A priority alert is dispatched to the Lead Manager’s Manager Action Centre.
The system presents mandatory Overdue Intervention options:

Option 1: Grant a deadline extension with a mandatory business justification (e.g., “Awaiting registered lease copy from Mumbai sub-registrar”).
Option 2: Reassign to an available team member after inspecting workload capacity.
Option 3: Escalate to Senior Manager for cross-departmental resource deployment.
Option 4: Scope reduction / direct guidance to the employee.
A Manager cannot simply click “+1 Day” without entering a formal justification. This justification is permanently recorded in the audit trail.
3.5 The Reassignment Protocol
Reassignment is a controlled workflow, not a casual drag-and-drop:
Manager selects a task and clicks “Reassign Task”.
The system displays the Team Bandwidth Radar, showing current task loads of all team members.
Manager selects a standardised reassignment reason: Employee Capacity/Overload, Medical/Approved Leave, Skillset Required, or Client Vertical Shift.
Manager writes a mandatory handover note detailing what was completed and what remains.
Indelible audit log entry records the transfer. The previous employee is relieved of deadline liability, and the new employee receives an urgent handover alert.
3.6 The Quality Review & Correction Cycle
Managerial review is never a blunt “Approve” or “Reject”. Rejection initiates a collaborative correction cycle:
Employee submits deliverables (files + notes). Task moves to “Waiting for Review”.
Manager reviews against quality criteria.
If approved: Task marked “Completed”. Performance points awarded.
If changes required: Manager returns the submission with itemised deficiency notes. Task re-enters “In Progress (Revision 2)”.
Employee implements corrections and resubmits with a revision changelog.
Manager conducts final QA approval. Task formally closed.
3.7 The Immutable Audit History Component
Every critical object in IOTS (Tasks, User Accounts, Geofences, Leave Requests, Royalty Settlements) contains an embedded Audit History Ledger.
Immutable Ledger Rules:
Append-Only: Audit records can never be updated, overwritten, or deleted by any user, including Super Admins.
Deterministic Attribution: Every entry records Actor ID, Actor Name, Active Role, Timestamp (ISO-8601 to the millisecond), Origin IP, and Geolocation Context.
Before & After Delta: Records precisely what changed (e.g., Priority: Normal → Critical).
Source Distinction: Differentiates between User-Initiated Actions and System-Automated Actions.
Each audit entry displays: Actor, Role, Timestamp, Action, Object/Resource, Previous Value, New Value, Reason/Comment, and Source (User Action / System Action). The component is strictly read-only and supports filtering by date, actor, action type, and resource.

4.2 The Geolocation Engine & Office Perimeter Geofencing
When an employee enters their credentials on the unified login screen, the system executes a three-step security handshake:
Credential Validation: Verifies Employee ID and Password against the internal directory.
Hardware GPS Query: Requests the browser’s Hardware GPS API for latitude and longitude coordinates.
Reverse Geocoding & Perimeter Check: Resolves the coordinates to a human-readable address (via BigDataCloud API), then compares the distance against the assigned office’s approved geofence perimeter using the Haversine formula.

If the employee is within the approved office radius (e.g., 100 metres), the session is tagged “Office / On-Premise” with a green badge. If outside the office perimeter, the session is tagged “Remote / Off-Site” with a calm amber pill and an optional reason memo—the system does NOT block them or treat them as a criminal.
4.3 Handling Remote & Unknown Locations: Dignity Over Fear
The IOTS philosophy:
No frightening error screens. If an employee is outside the office perimeter, they are not blocked.
Contextual Tagging: A calm message states “We notice you are signing in from outside your designated office perimeter. Your session has been marked as Remote / Off-Site.”
Optional Reason Memo: The employee can enter a quick note (e.g., “Working from home with prior approval”).
Manager Notification: The Lead Manager sees a subtle orange pin next to the employee’s name on their live team radar.
4.4 Attendance & Work Hours Computation
IOTS eliminates paper registers and manual time tracking:
Check-In Timestamp: Captured automatically upon first successful login of the day.
Check-Out Timestamp: Captured when the user clicks “Check-Out & Logoff”.
Net Work Hours: Active productive time = (Check-Out – Check-In) – Total Break Durations.
Standard Shift: 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM (9.0 Gross Hours / 8.0 Net Productive Hours).
Grace Period: 15 minutes. Arrivals up to 9:45 AM marked on-time.
Late Arrivals: Logins after 9:45 AM flagged. Three unexcused late arrivals per month trigger automated HR and Lead Manager notification.
4.5 Employee Self-Service Request Portal
Employees manage their work-life schedules through five request categories:
Leave Requests: Casual Leave (CL), Sick Leave (SL), Earned/Privilege Leave (PL), and Compensatory Off.
Short Permission Requests: 1 to 2 hours for doctor appointments, personal emergencies, or government office visits.
Scheduled Remote Work Requests: Prior approval to work from home for specific days.
Task Clarification / Guidance Requests: Urgent 15-minute sync with the Lead Manager.
Operational Resource Requests: Hardware, software licences, commercial portal credits, or conference stationery.
4.6 Security Auditing & Chairman Anomaly Cockpit
The Chairman, Super Admin, and IT System Analyst access the Master Security & Location Audit Centre. Automated anomaly detection covers:
Impossible Travel Velocity: Login in Hyderabad, then same account logs in from Dubai 45 minutes later. System suspends the second session and dispatches urgent alerts.
Ghost Logins & Session Hijacking: Active sessions are bound to cryptographically signed browser tokens. Copied cookies on a different browser are immediately invalidated.
Unapproved Remote Clusters: More than 5 consecutive off-site days without approved leave triggers an administrative flag.
Concurrent Multi-Device Logins: Simultaneous sessions from different IP subnets raise a security investigation event.
Every login event is preserved with its reverse-geocoded address, GPS coordinates, public IP address, ISP provider, and browser user-agent.

5. CIRIL Commercial Operations & Revenue Architecture
5.1 The CIRIL Syndication Model
CIRIL operates as an apex clearinghouse and national alliance of certified commercial real estate advisory firms (Member Companies). The platform provides national umbrella coverage, standardised institutional proposal templates, trusted dispute resolution, proprietary market intelligence, and automated fee distribution.
5.2 The 4-Tier Geographic Hierarchy
Country → Zones (North, South, East, West, Central) → States (Regulatory Jurisdictions) → Cities (Metropolitan Commercial Centres) → Member Companies → Advisory Associates.
5.3 The 6-Phase End-to-End Transaction Lifecycle
Mandate Origination (Lead Giver Stage): A Member Company in City A secures a mandate from an enterprise client. The broker logs the mandate with comprehensive parameters—space category, vertical type, required area, target budget, and technical specifications.
Central Clearance & Destination Handoff: CIRIL HQ verifies the mandate and routes it to an accredited Member Company in the destination city (Lead Receiver). The recipient must formally Accept or Reject within 24 hours.
Sourcing & Institutional Proposals: The Lead Receiver sources inventory and generates standardised presentations—a First-Cut Proposal (comparison matrix) and a Detailed Institutional Proposal (exhaustive building report).
Site Inspections & 7-Stage Negotiation: The deal advances through Basic Prospecting → Site Inspection → Shortlisting → Commercial Offer → Term Sheet → Legal Drafting → Agreement Execution.
Deal Conclusion & Financial Lock: Upon execution of the registered lease deed or sale deed, confirmed transaction metrics are locked.
Fee Apportionment & Royalty Settlement: The system calculates gross brokerage, applies the member split, extracts CIRIL’s 7.5% corporate royalty, and dispatches official conclusion statements.
5.4 Fee Structure & 7.5% Royalty Engine
Base Brokerage Calculation:
Commercial Lease: Gross Brokerage = 1 Month (30 Days) Rental Value.
Outright Sale / Acquisition: Gross Brokerage = 1.00% of Total Transaction Consideration.

Inter-Member Split:
Lead Giver (LG) Share: 30% of Gross Brokerage (for origination and client relationship management).
Lead Receiver (LR) Share: 70% of Gross Brokerage (for local sourcing, inspections, negotiations, and closing).

CIRIL Corporate Royalty = 7.5% of Total Gross Brokerage, deducted proportionally from both LG (7.5% of LG share) and LR (7.5% of LR share).

7. Employee Performance, Reward & Recognition System
7.1 The Philosophy of Objective Recognition
In conventional organisations, bonuses and awards are decided through subjective impressions, recency bias, or office politics. This damages morale. The CIRIL IOTS Reward System establishes complete objectivity and transparency. Because every task, delivery timestamp, review cycle, and attendance record is captured accurately, recognition is calculated through transparent mathematical scoring. Every employee can see their live performance score and reward trajectory daily.

Continuous Qualification: An employee must achieve Gold Tier (score ≥ 90) in three consecutive calendar months (e.g., October, November, December).
The Reset Covenant: If an employee achieves Gold in Month 1 and Month 2, but dips to Silver (85) in Month 3, the consecutive streak counter resets to zero.

7.6 Anti-Burnout Safeguards
No Midnight Heroics Required: Points are capped at standard shift durations. Working 14-hour days does not grant extra points.
Transparent Dispute Resolution: If an employee feels their score was unfairly affected by external blockers, they can submit a 1-click Score Review Request to the Senior Manager.
Open Self-Inspection: Employees can click on any score component to view the exact math and underlying records.

10. Core System Principles & Operational Guarantees
Complex Underneath, Simple on Top: The underlying data model tracks sub-metre GPS coordinates, millisecond audit events, and intricate revenue splits. But the user interface remains clean, calm, and self-explanatory.
Zero Passive Task Drift: A task can never simply “expire” into oblivion. When a deadline passes, the system immediately demands manager review and structured resolution.
Immutable Accountability (Audit by Design): Every major operational action writes an indelible entry into the audit trail. Audit logs cannot be altered or deleted by any user, including Super Admins.
Equity and Transparency: Performance is evaluated using verifiable operational metrics rather than subjective opinions. Every employee can view their own scorecards in real time.
Security with Dignity: Location verification protects corporate integrity and confirms workplace safety, not punitive surveillance. Remote and off-site work is seamlessly accommodated.
Unified Login, Multi-Role Flexibility: Single corporate login for all 11 roles. Employees with dual responsibilities switch workspace contexts instantly without re-authentication.
Every Chart Answers a Business Question: No decorative charts. Every visualisation in the Intelligence & Analytical Layer is designed to answer a specific operational question.
Constructive Correction, Never Punitive Termination: Managerial rejection of deliverables does not kill the task. It initiates a structured, traceable revision cycle with collaborative feedback.
